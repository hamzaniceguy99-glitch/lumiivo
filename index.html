How we work

A coach, not an agency

Be careful in this industry. Anyone charging to represent you, guaranteeing auditions, or selling a demo package before hearing you read is taking your money, not building your career.

Lumiivo sells coaching only. We don't submit you anywhere, take no commission, and will tell you plainly when you're not ready for a demo — which is the advice that actually saves people money.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you get me voice-over work?

No. We are not an agency or casting service, we submit you nowhere, and we take no commission. We coach the performance and the setup.

What equipment do I need to start?

A USB microphone in the $100–200 range and a quiet space. Don't buy more until your reads justify it — we'll tell you when.

Is my voice good enough?

Almost certainly a wrong question. Casting hires for authenticity and direction-taking far more often than for a 'nice voice'.

How long before I book work?

Usually years rather than months, with consistent auditioning. Anyone quoting a faster timeline is selling something.

Should I buy a demo package?

Not before you can consistently deliver good reads. A demo made too early actively hurts you, and we'll say so if that's where you are.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Want honest notes on a read?

Thirty seconds of copy and twenty minutes of feedback. Free, and specific.
