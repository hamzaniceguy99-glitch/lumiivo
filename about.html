How we work

Read, listen back, adjust

The first habit to break is performing. New readers push, smile audibly and add gravity nobody asked for, because that's what voice-over sounded like thirty years ago.

Fixing it is a weekly loop: real copy, a recording, and notes specific enough to act on. Listening back critically is the skill that keeps improving you long after coaching ends.

The technical side is simpler than people fear — a quiet room and correct levels clear the bar. And the business side mostly requires honesty about timelines, which this industry is short of.

Our principles

Three beliefs behind the method

Talk, don't announce

One person, one thought per line.

Listen back every time

The recording tells you what your ears in the moment can't.

No agency, no promises

We never submit you anywhere or guarantee bookings.
