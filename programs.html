The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 12 weeks, each built on audio you record every week.

Program 01 · Sound like a person

Reads & interpretation

Good reads come from knowing exactly who you're talking to and what you want them to feel. We work on marking up copy, on the one thought that drives each line, and on the habit of listening back critically — which is where most of the progress actually happens.

  • 12 weeks
  • Beginners welcome
  • 4 hours a week
  • Any microphone

Program 02 · Sound that passes

Home studio

Casting rejects noisy audio before hearing the performance. We fix the room first with blankets and a closet rather than purchases, set levels properly, and cover the editing and file naming that make you easy to work with.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 3 hours a week
  • Mic, interface or USB

Start this program

Program 03 · Worth sending

Your demo

A demo made too early is the most common money wasted in this business. We first work out what you'd realistically be cast as, then build a short demo from scripts that suit it. We'll tell you honestly if you're not ready — waiting six months is cheaper than a demo nobody plays past ten seconds.

  • 10 weeks
  • After reads program
  • 5 hours a week
  • A working home setup

Program 04 · A sustainable routine

Auditions & business

Voice-over is a numbers game with a long lead time. We build an audition routine you can sustain alongside a job, cover how rates and usage work so you don't underprice, and set expectations honestly — this takes years, and anyone promising otherwise is selling something.
